# Privacy policy: 1-Minute Sleep Journal

*Last updated: November 13, 2026*

1-Minute Sleep Journal is a free, open-source sleep diary. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics
and no tracking. The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data.

## Your journal stays on your device

Everything you enter (your nights, notes, tags, dreams, routines and settings) is stored only on
your device: in the app on your phone, or in your browser's own storage if you use the web version.
It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) deletes your journal. You can export
it at any time as a spreadsheet (.xlsx or .csv) or a report (PDF, web page or Markdown), and it is up to
you who you share those files with.

## Optional: your own Google Sheet

If you choose to connect a Google Sheet (Settings → Google Sheet), the app sends your journal
directly from your device to a script inside **your own** Google Sheet, and reads changes back from
it. That copy is stored in your Google account, under Google's terms and privacy policy. The app never
sees your Google login; it only uses the web address and secret key you paste in. You can disconnect
at any time, and the developer has no access to your sheet.

## What the app connects to, and why

The app works offline. It only goes online for these:

- **App updates (Expo).** To get the newest version of the app, it checks for updates on Expo's
  servers, which handle the app's updates. These checks contain no journal data.
- **New Android versions (GitHub).** The Android app installed from GitHub (not from a store) checks
  the project's GitHub page once a day for a newer version, unless you switch that off in Settings.
  This check contains no journal data.
- **Your Google Sheet,** only if you connect one (above).
- **The website** is served by Expo's hosting. Like any website, its servers see the usual request
  details (such as your IP address) when your browser loads the page; your journal is not sent.

Reminders are scheduled on your device by your device; they don't go through any server.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
